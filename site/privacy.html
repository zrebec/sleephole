Privacy Policy

SleepHole · last updated October 12, 2026

SleepHole collects no data about you. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer of SleepHole never receives your nights, your town, your sleep data or anything else from the app.

The rest of this page says where your data is kept and which services the app talks to.

On your device

Your nights, your town, coins, achievements, settings and your wake code are stored on your iPhone.

Your own iCloud (optional)

When “Sync through iCloud” is on, your nights, coins and settings – including the wake code and the city you chose – are also kept as files in your own iCloud, so that your town is the same on all your devices. Only you can access them; the developer cannot.

Data from Apple Health is never put into these files. You can switch sync off in the app’s Settings at any time.

Apple Health (only if you allow it)

If you switch on “Show when you fell asleep”, SleepHole reads your sleep data from Apple Health to show when you fell asleep and how long you slept. It only reads and never writes anything to Health.

What it reads stays on your device. It is not sent to anyone and it is not put into the app’s iCloud sync.

Weather and city search (only if you choose a city)

SleepHole never asks for your location. To show the sun, the moon and the weather, it sends the coordinates of the city you chose in Settings to a weather service: Apple WeatherKit, or MET Norway (api.met.no) when the first one does not answer. As with any internet request, the service sees your IP address.

Searching for a city uses Apple Maps, so the text you type goes to Apple.

Their own policies: Apple · MET Norway

Notifications and alarms

Reminders, warnings and alarms are created on your device. There is no server that sends them.

Backups

The app keeps a backup file on your device (Files → On My iPhone → SleepHole) and lets you export one. It holds your nights, town and settings, including sleep values read from Apple Health. The file goes only where you put it.

Like the data of every app, SleepHole’s data is part of the backups you make of your whole iPhone, for example iCloud Backup or a backup to a computer.

Deleting your data

  • On the device: delete the app. Everything it stored on the device is removed with it.
  • In iCloud: switch off “Sync through iCloud” in the app to stop syncing. To remove what is already there, open iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Storage → SleepHole.
  • Apple Health: switch off “Show when you fell asleep” in the app, or take the permission back in the Health app → your profile → Apps → SleepHole.

The developer holds none of your data, so there is nothing to request or delete on the developer’s side.

Children

SleepHole collects no data from anyone, children included.
